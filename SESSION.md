## 2026-07-29 — VideoSpeed: ad auto-skip via chrome.debugger
Iterated on the `videospeed` extension's YouTube ad-skip feature through several rounds of live debugging (wrong `.html5-video-player` selector, YouTube resetting `playbackRate` during ads, an overly-broad ad-detection selector forcing 4x on normal page load).
